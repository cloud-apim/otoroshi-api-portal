# Plans

The keys of a portal are keys of the **plans of the API** (`plans` of `apis.otoroshi.io/Api`).

```json
{
  "id": "pro",
  "name": "Pro",
  "description": "For production: higher quotas, on approval.",
  "status": "published",
  "access_mode_configuration_type": "apikey",
  "access_mode_configuration": {},
  "validation": { "kind": "manual" },
  "visibility": { "kind": "public" },
  "rate_limiting": {
    "strategy": {
      "id": "LegacyThrottlingStrategyConfig",
      "quota": { "window": 100, "daily": 100000, "monthly": 2000000 }
    }
  }
}
```

| Field | Description |
|---|---|
| `status` | `published` to show the plan; any other status hides it |
| `visibility` | `{ "kind": "private" }` hides the plan from the portal |
| `access_mode_configuration_type` | `apikey`: developers take keys of the plan. The other kinds (`keyless`, `jwt`, `mtls`, `oauth2-*`) are shown on the home, no key is taken of them |
| `access_mode_configuration` | The settings of the keys of the plan (a name pattern, restrictions…) |
| `rate_limiting` | The quotas of the keys: calls per window, per day, per month |
| `validation` | `{ "kind": "auto" }`: a key at once. `{ "kind": "manual" }`: the key waits for an approval |
| `tags`, `metadata` | Applied to the keys of the plan |

## On the portal

The published plans are cards on the home of the portal, with their quotas. The `apikey` ones enable
**My keys**, where they are cards too: their description, their quotas, and whether the key comes
at once or on approval.

![The plans of a new key](/img/screenshots/plan-cards.png)

## What a key is in Otoroshi

Taking a key of a plan creates a **subscription** to it (`apis.otoroshi.io/ApiSubscription`, owned by
the email of the developer), and Otoroshi makes its **API key** from the plan: its quotas, its tags,
its metadata, authorized on the API. With a `manual` plan, the subscription is `pending` and its key
disabled until the subscription is confirmed in Otoroshi.
